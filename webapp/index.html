METHODOLOGY & DATA

How the Model Works

An ensemble ML approach combining satellite imagery, weather station telemetry, and terrain data to predict fire risk with high accuracy.

🛰

NASA Satellite Data

Raw imagery and derived products from NASA's AppEEARS platform, providing multi-spectral data critical for vegetation and moisture analysis.

  • MODIS Terra/Aqua LST (Land Surface Temperature)
  • Landsat-8 NDVI (Vegetation Index)
  • MODIS active fire product (MOD14)
  • Surface reflectance bands 1–7
🌡

Weather Station Network

Historical and real-time telemetry from BC's network of weather stations, merged with NASA satellite observations by date and location.

  • Temperature, humidity, wind speed
  • Precipitation accumulation (daily / 7-day)
  • Drought Code (DC) and Fine Fuel Moisture
  • Danger Rating from BC Wildfire Service
🏔

Geographic Features

Elevation, slope, and aspect from BC digital terrain models layered with historical fire perimeters as ground-truth labels.

  • DEM elevation (metres)
  • Slope steepness (degrees)
  • Aspect / solar exposure
  • Distance to nearest historical fire
🤖

ML Ensemble Pipeline

Multiple classifiers tested and combined into an ensemble that outperformed any individual model on held-out validation data.

  • Logistic Regression (baseline)
  • Random Forest (feature importance)
  • Gradient Boosting (XGBoost)
  • Soft-voting Ensemble (best accuracy)
✅

Ground Truth Validation

Model predictions validated against historical BC wildfire records, ensuring the classifier generalizes beyond training data.

  • BC Wildfire Service historical ignitions
  • Cross-referenced with CWFIS (national system)
  • Temporal holdout: 2020–2022 test set
  • Spatial holdout: independent fire seasons
📐

Why It's Accurate

The combination of multi-modal inputs and an ensemble voting strategy reduces individual model biases, producing robust, well-calibrated risk scores.

  • 24 engineered features from 3 data domains
  • Class-weighted training to handle imbalance
  • 89.4% overall accuracy on test set
  • AUC-ROC of 0.932 (strong discrimination)
